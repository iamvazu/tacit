Your readiness

    Security & privacy

    Built so a privacy lawyer can say yes

    Read-only, scoped, revocable

    We request the narrowest access each system allows, limited to the channels, folders or date ranges you pick. Revoke it in one click from your admin console.

    Two-pass de-identification

    Automated detection of names, emails, phone numbers, IDs, addresses and account numbers, then human review of a sample from every batch. Names are replaced with consistent placeholders so the work still reads correctly.

    Isolated processing

    Each client's data sits in its own encrypted workspace with access logging. Raw data never goes to a buyer. Buyers see redacted previews before purchase.

    Contracts that hold up

    Mutual NDA, data processing agreement, and a licence that limits use to training and evaluation, bans re-identification and requires deletion on termination.

    Questions

    What sellers ask first

    Will my customers' or employees' data end up in an AI model?

    Not in identifiable form. We remove names, contact details, IDs and account numbers before anything leaves processing, and you review a redacted preview before any sale. Where the law requires notice to employees or customers, we give you templates and wait until it's done.

    How fast do I get paid?

    Most deals take four to eight weeks from first call to payout, depending on the rights review and the buyer's acceptance testing. Funds are held in escrow from signing, so payment is released as soon as the buyer accepts delivery.

    Who are the buyers?

    AI labs and companies training specialist models. We name the buyer and their intended use before you approve a sale. We don't sell to data resellers.

    What if my data turns out not to be sellable?

    Then you pay nothing. Our fee only applies to licences that close.

    Does this expose trade secrets?

    You choose the scope. Pricing, product roadmaps, source code or specific folders can be excluded entirely, and we redact named products and clients on request.

    Can I sell the same data to more than one lab?

    Yes, by default licences are non-exclusive. If a buyer wants exclusivity, they pay a premium and you decide whether to accept it.

    Do my employees get anything?

    If your experts annotate decisions, they are paid a consulting rate per hour on top of the licence. Many sellers also share part of the licence with the team.
